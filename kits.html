Design Workbooks: emergency PDFs

What to do if the app stops working for more than a lesson or two. Short enough to print. (Updated 26 Nov 2026, app 2026-11-26.1900.)

Every night (about 2 am) the app copies all work that changed into Backups, keeping the last two copies of each workbook. It also makes a PDF of each open workbook for every student whose work changed: their answers, photos, plan and process journal, with the date it was saved. Each student has their own folder of PDFs. Everything stays private in the teacher's Google Drive until a teacher shares it. Sharing never sends an email.

For teachers

When to share: only when the app has been down for more than a day or two, or before a holiday if you're worried. Students can read their work in the PDFs. They can't change it there.

How to share (pick one):

  1. In the app: ⚙ Settings › App › Backups and emergency PDFs. Type all, a class (e.g. 8A) or students' emails, then press Share (no emails).
  2. In the Settings sheet (works even when the web app doesn't): menu Design Workbooks › Backups and emergency PDFs › Share PDFs… (no emails).
  3. By hand in Google Drive (if Apps Script itself is down): open the Emergency PDFs folder (next to the Settings sheet), then the class folder. Right-click a student's folder (Name – workbook PDFs), choose Share, add that student as a Viewer, untick "Notify people", press Share. One student per folder.

Tell students in class or on Classroom: "Your PDFs are in Google Drive › Shared with me."

Afterwards: Settings › App › Stop sharing, or the sheet menu Stop sharing PDFs. (Folders you shared by hand: remove the student in Drive's Share dialog.)

Turn it on once: sheet menu Design Workbooks › Backups and emergency PDFs › Turn on nightly backups and PDFs, or the button in Settings › App. Make backups and PDFs now starts straight away. PDFs take a few seconds each, so the first run for a whole school carries on by itself for a while.

Also kept, private: Emergency kits (one HTML page per student, a spare copy) and Backups / <workbook> / <student or group> (the last two copies of each workbook.json; ask whoever looks after the app to put one back).

For students

  1. Open Google Drive and go to Shared with me.
  2. Open the folder your name – workbook PDFs.
  3. Open the PDF for the workbook you need. It works on a phone, a tablet or a laptop. You can download it to keep a copy.
  4. It shows your answers, photos, plan and process journal, with the date it was saved. In group workbooks you see your group's work and your own parts.
  5. You can't change it. Keep working in a Google Doc or on paper, and put the new work into the app when it is back.
