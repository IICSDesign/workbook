Design Workbooks: emergency kits

What to do if the app stops working for more than a lesson or two. Short enough to print.

Every night (about 2 am) the app copies all work that changed into Backups, keeping the last two copies of each workbook. It also remakes one emergency kit per student: a single page with all their work, photos, plan and process journal. Kits stay private in the teacher's Google Drive until a teacher shares them. Sharing never sends an email.

For teachers

When to share kits: only when the app has been down for more than a day or two, or before a holiday if you're worried. Students can read their work in the kit. They can't change it there yet.

How to share (pick one):

  1. In the app: ⚙ Settings › App › Backups and emergency kits. Type all, a class (e.g. 8A) or students' emails, then press Share (no emails).
  2. In the Settings sheet (works even when the web app doesn't): menu Design Workbooks › Backups and emergency kits › Share kits… (no emails).
  3. By hand in Google Drive (if Apps Script itself is down): open the Emergency kits folder (next to the Settings sheet), open the class folder, select a student's file, press Share, add that student as a Viewer, untick "Notify people", press Share. One student per file: each kit holds one student's work.

Tell students in class or on Classroom: "Your emergency copy is in Google Drive › Shared with me."

Afterwards: Settings › App › Stop sharing, or the sheet menu Stop sharing kits. (Kits you shared by hand: remove the student in Drive's Share dialog.)

Turn it on once: sheet menu Design Workbooks › Backups and emergency kits › Turn on nightly backups and kits, or the button in Settings › App. Make backups and kits now makes them straight away (for a whole school it carries on by itself for a few minutes).

Backups: the folder Backups / <workbook> / <student or group> holds the last two copies of each workbook.json. To put an old copy back, ask whoever looks after the app (it is the same file the app saves, so it can be swapped in).

For students

  1. Open Google Drive and go to Shared with me.
  2. Find Design Workbooks – your name – emergency copy.
  3. Laptop: download it, then double-click it. It opens in your browser and works without the internet. Phone: open it, or download it and open it from Files.
  4. It shows all your work: your answers, photos, plan and process journal, with the date it was saved.
  5. You can't change it. Keep working in a Google Doc or on paper, and put the new work into the app when it is back.
